# Prompty dla Figma AI do Projektu Strony Fotografa

Poniżej przedstawiono zestaw promptów, które możesz wykorzystać w narzędziach AI do projektowania (np. Musho, Relume, Framer/Figma AI) w Figmie, aby wygenerować profesjonalny projekt strony internetowej dla lokalnego fotografa. Prompty są oparte na wcześniej opracowanej strukturze i uwzględniają kluczowe aspekty wizualne i funkcjonalne.

## Ogólny Prompt (Main Prompt)

> "Design a modern, professional, and visually stunning website for a local photographer specializing in [e.g., wedding, portrait, commercial] photography. The site should be elegant, clean, and focused on showcasing high-quality imagery, but not overly minimalist. It needs to convey trust, expertise, and a personal touch. Include clear calls to action and ensure a seamless user experience. The primary goal is to attract new clients and present the photographer's portfolio and services effectively. The design should be responsive and optimized for fast loading. Use a sophisticated color palette and elegant typography. Avoid overly trendy or distracting elements. The website should feel premium and approachable."

## Prompty dla Konkretnych Sekcji (Section-Specific Prompts)

### 1. Strona Główna (Homepage)

> "Design the homepage for a photographer's website. Feature a full-width, high-impact hero section with a captivating image or subtle video background, a clear headline (e.g., 'Capture Your Unforgettable Moments'), and a prominent call-to-action button (e.g., 'View Portfolio'). Below the hero, include a brief introduction to the photographer's style and specialization, followed by a curated selection of 3-5 top portfolio images linking to respective galleries. Add a section for recent client testimonials and a subtle footer with contact information and social media links. Ensure fast loading and a clean layout."

### 2. Portfolio / Galerie (Portfolio / Galleries)

> "Create a portfolio section for a photographer's website. Design a clean, grid-based layout for image galleries, allowing for categorization (e.g., 'Weddings', 'Portraits', 'Family', 'Commercial'). Each gallery should open to a dedicated page with a title, a brief description of the project/session, and a larger display of high-resolution images. Implement smooth transitions between images and a subtle lightbox feature. Ensure images load quickly and are presented beautifully. Include navigation back to the main portfolio page and a call to action to 'Book a Session' or 'Inquire'."

### 3. Usługi i Cennik (Services & Pricing)

> "Design a 'Services & Pricing' page for a photographer. Present the services clearly, perhaps using cards or distinct blocks for each package (e.g., 'Wedding Packages', 'Portrait Sessions'). For each service, include a concise description of what's included, estimated duration, and a 'starting from' price or a full price list. Use clear headings and bullet points for readability. Add a call to action for each service to 'Learn More' or 'Request a Custom Quote'. Maintain a professional and transparent tone."

### 4. O Mnie / O Fotografce (About Me)

> "Design an 'About Me' page for a photographer. Feature a professional, friendly photo of the photographer. Include a compelling narrative about their passion for photography, their unique style, and what drives them. Share a brief personal story or philosophy. This section should build trust and connect with potential clients on a personal level. Incorporate subtle design elements that reflect the photographer's brand. Add links to social media profiles."

### 5. Kontakt (Contact)

> "Create a 'Contact' page for a photographer's website. Include a simple, user-friendly contact form with fields for Name, Email, Phone (optional), and Message. Provide alternative contact methods such as a direct email address and phone number. If applicable, include a map showing the studio location and business hours. Add links to social media profiles. Ensure the design is clean and encourages easy communication."

## Prompty dla Elementów Projektowych (Design Element Prompts)

### Typografia

> "Suggest an elegant and readable typography pairing for a professional photography website. Use a sophisticated serif font for headings and a clean, modern sans-serif font for body text. Ensure good contrast and readability across all screen sizes."

### Paleta Kolorów

> "Propose a sophisticated and timeless color palette for a photography website. Focus on neutral tones (grays, creams, whites) with one or two subtle accent colors (e.g., deep teal, warm gold, muted navy) that evoke professionalism and elegance. The palette should complement diverse photographic styles without overpowering the images."

### Responsywność

> "Ensure all design elements and layouts are fully responsive and adapt seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Prioritize mobile-first design principles for optimal user experience on smaller screens."

## Wskazówki Dodatkowe

*   **Iteruj:** Narzędzia AI najlepiej działają w procesie iteracyjnym. Zacznij od ogólnych promptów, a następnie doprecyzowuj poszczególne sekcje i elementy.
*   **Referencje Wizualne:** Jeśli masz konkretne przykłady stron, które Ci się podobają, możesz dodać do promptu: "Inspiruj się stylem [URL strony referencyjnej]".
*   **Język:** Używaj precyzyjnego języka, aby AI mogło jak najlepiej zrozumieć Twoje intencje.

Powodzenia w projektowaniu!
